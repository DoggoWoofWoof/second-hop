Second Hop shows benchmark passages collected from English Wikipedia. The
article link beside each passage identifies its source and provides access to
its contributor history. The passages preserve the text of the saved benchmark
snapshot, which may differ from today's article. Wikipedia text is available
under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyrights

adds the matching names verified against the recorded named-entity link weights.
