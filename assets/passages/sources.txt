Second Hop shows benchmark passages collected from English Wikipedia. The
article link beside each passage identifies its source and provides access to
its contributor history. The passages preserve the text of the saved benchmark
snapshot, which may differ from today's article. Wikipedia text is available
under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike.
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyrights

The demo uses the canonical movie entity names, selected development questions
and answers, and a subset of the movie relations. It normalizes presentation
capitalization and replaces benchmark markup with clickable entities.

The trained run uses benchmark corpus titles, saved passage text and the subset
of edges used by each recorded candidate pool. Where names can be recovered,
the shared names are checked against the original named-entity link weights.
