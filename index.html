        Data and methods

        First search

        Dense and sparse retrievers order the passages by meaning and words. Reciprocal rank fusion combines those orders.

        Pool replay

        Each selected search hit contributes its direct neighbours from the enabled link types. Duplicate passages count once. The graph shows the current seeds, their neighbours, and the connections between them.

        Ranking replay

        The demo adds a bonus to each neighbour based on its best connected search hit. The bonus equals that hit’s fused score times the number of enabled link types on the connection, divided by four, times the chosen boost. The saved MLP and GNN runs used a fixed shortlist and seed 0.
