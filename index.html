    Data and methods

    First search

    Dense and sparse retrievers order the passages by meaning and words. Reciprocal rank fusion combines those orders.

    Pool replay

    Each selected search hit contributes its direct neighbours from the enabled link types. Duplicate passages count once. The evidence map draws a subset around the first five hits.

    Ranking replay

    The saved MLP and GNN outputs use a fixed research shortlist and seed 0. The interactive pool shows what a one-hop expansion would admit at different budgets.

    Benchmark sources and link definitions

    The six questions come from development snapshots of 2WikiMultihopQA, HotpotQA, and MuSiQue. The HippoRAG 2 paper documents the passage collections. The full-corpus coverage and timing charts each link to their own larger development runs.
