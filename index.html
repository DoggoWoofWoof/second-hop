How it works

Start with five

The first search combines rankings by words and by meaning. Its first five passages are the starting points.

Follow one link

A passage comes into reach when it connects directly to a starting point through an enabled link type.

Check the evidence

Each benchmark marks the passages that support the answer. The map counts how many of those passages are in reach.

Data and link definitions

The playground uses six selected development questions from 2WikiMultihopQA, HotpotQA, and MuSiQue. It replays recorded first-search ranks and links between the passages shown. Positions on the map are schematic. The original search ranks stay fixed when you toggle links.

Hyperlinks
Article links supplied by the benchmark corpus.
Title rules
Passages that mention another passage’s title or come from the same article.
Shared names
Passages with a named entity in common.
Semantic
Links to the nearest passages by meaning.

Passage excerpts are by Wikipedia contributors, as distributed in the benchmark datasets, under CC BY-SA 4.0.
