            Research model shapes

            MLP: Two 128-wide GELU layers score each passage, with a learned contribution from its standardized fused score.

            GNN: A 64-wide state passes through three typed neighbour updates. The updates combine per-family means and question-conditioned attention.

            Next-hop scorer: Fourteen anchor–candidate inputs feed two 32-wide layers and a bilinear score before normalization within the anchor’s neighbours.

              Data and methods

              First search

              Dense and sparse retrievers order the passages by meaning and words. Reciprocal rank fusion combines those orders.

              Candidate pools

              The trained run replays each dataset’s frozen search budget and bounded seed expansion. The Walk lab lets you change the graph and restart settings in a separate PageRank experiment.

              Trained comparisons

              MLP (zrc) and GNN (zsp) are the J5 p@swa checkpoints. Each scores every candidate in the same pool. The GNN adds one learned correction from the scores of its neighbours.
